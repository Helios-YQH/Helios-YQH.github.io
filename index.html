Welcome — I'm Yi Hou (厚熠), a second-year Artificial Intelligence undergraduate at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, Beijing.

My path into deep learning started with computer vision: real-time hand-gesture interfaces with MediaPipe, 3D reconstruction with COLMAP and 3D Gaussian Splatting, and a from-scratch reproduction of StraightPCF for point-cloud denoising that placed in the national top 100 of the 6th Jittor AI Challenge.

From there I moved down the stack — re-implementing diffusion models from scratch (UNet vs. DiT, ε-prediction vs. rectified flow) — and then into the full language-modeling stack: byte-level BPE, a decoder-only Transformer, Triton attention kernels, distributed training (DDP / ZeRO-1 / FSDP), a Common Crawl filtering pipeline, and post-training with SFT / DPO / GRPO. The work is public as llm-from-scratch: four projects, five technical reports, every number reproducible from raw run records.
